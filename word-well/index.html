how to play

Cast letter-stones onto the board to build interlocking words, crossword-style. The first word must cover the glowing star ✦.

Tap a stone, then tap the board. Tap a placed stone to take it back. Every word you form — across and down — must be a real word of 2+ letters.

Colored squares multiply new stones only: DL doubles a letter, TL triples it, DW doubles the word, TW triples it. Use all 7 stones in one turn for a +35 bingo.

Blank stones (·) become any letter you choose but score 0. You may swap stones (if 7+ remain) or pass instead of playing.

The game ends when someone plays their last stone with the well empty, or after 6 scoreless turns in a row.
