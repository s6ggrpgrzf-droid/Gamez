House Rules

How to Play

  • Match a tile to either open end of the chain.
  • Stuck? Tap the boneyard and draw until you can play.
  • Boneyard empty and still stuck? Knock.
  • Empty your hand to take the round — you score every pip left in the House's hand, plus the boneyard.
  • If both sides knock, fewest pips wins the difference.
  • First to 100 takes the match.
