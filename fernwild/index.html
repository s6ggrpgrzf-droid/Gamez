FIELD GUIDE

PLANT — tap an empty soil tile, pick a seed. Pine is quick, oak is mighty, berry bushes feed the small ones.

GROW — trees grow on their own: seed, sprout, sapling, adult, ancient, fallen trunk. Every stage houses someone new.

WELCOME — when the habitat is right, animals move in on their own. Tap them to see what they do.

WEAVE — plant mushrooms beside trees to thread the mycorrhizal web. Linked trees grow faster and the web earns its keep.
