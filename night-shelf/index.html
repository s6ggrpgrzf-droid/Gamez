THE NIGHT SHELF

a mahjong solitaire for the small hours

After closing, the shop belongs to its keeper. One hundred and forty-four curios stand on the counter in the old Turtle's shape, and by candlelight you take them down two at a time — a tile and its match, each one standing free: nothing resting on it, and a long side open to the air. Clear the shelf before the candle burns down, and the night is yours.

Dealt fair by construction — every shelf can be cleared, so a loss is never the deal's fault.
