Getting Around

Edmond Road westbound still closed

Both westbound lanes of Edmond Road between Broadway and Fretz Ave. stay closed in Phase 2 of the downtown intersection work, begun Nov 2 — concrete panels, signals, ADA and stormwater work, about four weeks in all. The city has posted no reopening notice yet.

Danforth: the daytime eastbound right lane stays closed, Chowning Ave. to Blackwelder Ave., for sewer repairs running about two weeks from Nov 16; the lane reopens at the end of each workday.

Rankin Street south of 15th was posted as a month-long closure from Oct 22 for pavement replacement, so expect it still shut.

Thanksgiving travel: the Turnpike Authority's holiday advisory flags bridge reconstruction on I-44 at Arcadia Lake — two lanes each way stay open, with lane shifts through spring 2027 — and intermittent lane shifts on the Kilpatrick Turnpike as it widens to six lanes.

New Around Town

Tru by Hilton breaks ground

2865 Progressive Drive, near Legacy at Covell. The city announced a Nov 4 groundbreaking for a 98-room Tru by Hilton — a $17 million investment with 25 to 30 jobs, expected to open in early 2028. It would be Edmond's first new hotel since the Hilton Garden Inn in 2018.
