Getting Around

Thanksgiving closures, then the usual roadwork

City offices closed Fri, Nov 27. Trash and recycling run a day late starting Thursday. Citylink runs a reduced schedule today — in-town routes 1–6 on the Saturday schedule, no 100X, and CAPS 9 AM–5 PM today and Saturday.

Edmond Road: both westbound lanes between Broadway and Fretz Ave. stay closed in Phase 2 of the downtown intersection work, begun Nov 2 — the city has posted no reopening notice yet.

Danforth: the daytime eastbound right lane stays closed, Chowning Ave. to Blackwelder Ave., for sewer repairs running about two weeks from Nov 16; the lane reopens at the end of each workday.

W. Hurd Street near the BNSF tracks may be fully closed at times for Edmond Electric utility work, expected to finish Jan. 20, 2027.

Thanksgiving travel: ODOT warns of lane restrictions and ramp closures statewide Thu, Nov 26, through Sun, Nov 29 — on I-44 at Arcadia Lake, two lanes each way stay open with lane shifts through spring 2027, and the Kilpatrick Turnpike has intermittent lane shifts as it widens to six lanes.

Have your say: the city launched its resident satisfaction survey on Nov 24 — the link is on the city's news page.
