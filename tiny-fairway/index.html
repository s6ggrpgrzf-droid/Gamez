Tiny Fairway

One finger. Twenty holes.

Drag back anywhere on the screen to aim — a dotted path shows where the ball will roll, bending with the slope. Release to shoot. Longer drag, longer shot.

Read the wind — the flag streams with it, streaks drift across the hole, and the gauge (top-right) shows direction and strength. Wind pushes putts around. Bank shots off the brick walls, time the windmill's blades, and hit ramps with speed to fly over trouble.

Read the break: the drifting white arrows show which way putts will curve — aim into the slope and let it feed the ball to the cup.

Spin: while aiming, drag a second finger up/down (or the mouse wheel) — topspin runs on, backspin bites. Curve: after release, drag sideways to bend the ball mid-flight. Overdrive: drag past 90% for a super shot; the shaking red line means it might go anywhere.

Power-ups: earn one per hole. ◼ air-brake kills the ball's speed mid-flight (tap PWR while it's moving), ◉ sticky stops the next landing dead, ↺ mulligan rewinds the whole hole. Race: you and 3 rivals putt at once — first in wins. Bump a gem-carrier to spill a gem. Relics: one rainbow shard hides on every hole, off the fast line.

Sink it in as few strokes as you can: match par for ★★★. Water costs a stroke. Sand is slow. ↩ takes back your last shot; after 20 strokes you can skip the hole. The turtle is harmless, but it will judge a bad hole.
