Tiny Fairway

One finger. Infinite holes.

Drag back anywhere on the screen to aim — a dotted arc shows where the ball will go. Release to shoot. Longer drag, longer shot.

Spin: while aiming, drag a second finger up/down (or the mouse wheel) — topspin runs on, backspin bites. Overdrive: drag past 90% for a super shot; the shaking red line means it might go anywhere.

Sink it in as few strokes as you can: match par for ★★★. Water costs a stroke. Sand is slow. ↩ takes back your last shot; after 20 strokes you can skip the hole. The turtle is harmless, but it will judge a bad hole.
