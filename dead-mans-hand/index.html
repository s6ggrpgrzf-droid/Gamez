HOW TO PLAY

  1. Build tableau columns down in alternating colors (red 7 on black 8).
  2. Only a King starts an empty column.
  3. Build the four foundations up by suit, Ace to King.
  4. Drag cards, or tap a card then its destination. Double-tap sends a card to its foundation.
  5. Tap the stock to draw. Tap it empty to redeal.
  6. Clear all four foundations to win. Undo is unlimited — no shame in it.
